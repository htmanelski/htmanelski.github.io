Modernity Becomes Antiquity

There will come a time when historians look back on our "modern" world and try to interpret what they find, much like we do with ancient sources. History suggests that our current digital medium will one day become obsolete and most communications will be lost. What will be preserved? This project offers a glimpse. With each visit, a public social media post, blog, or website is found at random — showing what future historians might see. What conclusions would they draw?
