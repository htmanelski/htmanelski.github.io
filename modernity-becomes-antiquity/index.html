The Late Electronic Age (c. 1990-2010 CE)

What remains offers mere glimpses into a lost world. The specimens below, recovered from the ruins of the Global Information Network, represent some of the only surviving texts from this enigmatic era. Scholars continue to debate their meaning, their authors, even their purpose.
