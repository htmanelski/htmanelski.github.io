Mars · SuperCam · 2024

Making elemental measurements on Mars more reliable.

SuperCam uses laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy to measure Martian chemistry from a distance. This work tested how target properties and observation geometry affect the plasma—and therefore the elemental abundances we infer.

Mars · ChemCam · 2023

Following compositional change through Gale crater’s sedimentary record.

ChemCam spectra capture systematic changes as Curiosity climbs through Mount Sharp. This project used active and passive observations to identify compositional variations among sedimentary units and connect them to environmental history.

My contributionLead author · spectral analysis · stratigraphic interpretation
